Volunteer Service Management App

An incident-reporting and response-coordination app for firefighters, built at Santa Clara University's EPIC Lab — I'm its primary developer.

Hydration Automation

FlutterNode.js/ExpressArduino C++LoRaNFC

An IoT platform for real-time monitoring of agricultural hydration systems, built at the EPIC Lab — I work across every tier of the stack.

I built the cross-platform Flutter app (NFC- and QR-based device onboarding, real-time sensor dashboards, localization, CI), wrote the embedded firmware (Arduino/C++ on SAMD21 with LoRa: ACK-gated transmission with offline queueing, NTP-disciplined clocks, OTA-refreshed device whitelists, battery telemetry), and co-developed the Express backend with server-side timestamping, deduplication, and passwordless magic-link sign-in.
